About HaramMute

HaramMute is a browser extension that removes background music from videos while preserving dialogue and speech.

It works like an adblocker, but for music. No complex software, no file uploads - just a browser extension that works.

How it works

When you click HaramMute on a video, we use AI-powered audio separation to isolate vocals from background music. The processing happens locally on your desktop for privacy and speed.
